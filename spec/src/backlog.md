# Backlog

This is the entry point for outstanding Notes work. Current behavior belongs to
its feature chapter; verification tables own requirement status and evidence.
Backlog entries describe missing implementation or qualification and retain the
original acceptance boundary. Historical receipts do not schedule current work.

- [Legacy plugin ports](backlog/legacy-plugin-ports.md)

[Server backlog](https://github.com/lapismd/lapis-server/blob/main/spec/src/backlog.md).
Tauri, native/provider and distribution follow-ups belong to the
[Desktop backlog](https://github.com/lapismd/lapis-desktop/blob/main/spec/src/backlog.md).
Those owners maintain their own status; Notes does not mirror their ledgers.
