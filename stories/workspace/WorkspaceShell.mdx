# Workspace shell

`@lapis-notes/workspace` mounts the API-owned design-core controller after the
consumer has loaded the vault and restored Lapis workspace persistence. The
stories use a real `App` and the built-in empty view. The notification center
is the sole static design-core shell plugin; Lapis and community plugins are
not loaded.
